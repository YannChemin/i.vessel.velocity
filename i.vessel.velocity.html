DESCRIPTION

i.vessel.velocity estimates the speed and heading of vessels from a single multispectral image of a push-broom sensor, by default the Sentinel-2 MSI. The MSI records each band at a slightly different time: the green band (B03) 0.527 s, the red band (B04) 1.005 s and the near-infrared band (B08) 0.264 s after the blue band (B02). The bands of a Level-2A product are orthorectified for the ground, so a static target sits at the same place in every band, while a moving vessel shifts by its velocity times the time lag (Heiselberg 2019). A vessel at 10 knots moves about 5 m, half a 10 m pixel, between B02 and B04.

For each point of the input vector map, usually the barycentres of detected vessels, the module:

  1. reads a small window of the four bands around the point;
  2. subtracts the local sea background (median of a ring around the hull) and computes the intensity-weighted barycentre of the bright blob in each band;
  3. fits position against acquisition time by least squares, which gives the velocity components, the standard error of the speed from the scatter of the four positions, and the heading as a bearing from grid north;
  4. compares the heading with the hull axis, if orient_column is given: a vessel under way moves along its long axis;
  5. for a moving vessel, compares the mean near-infrared brightness on a strip one to three hull lengths behind it with the same strip ahead: the wake trails behind, so a larger value behind supports the direction of the heading.

The output map is a copy of the input with these columns added:

Column Content
n_bands bands in which the vessel was found (3 or 4 to measure)
snr_min lowest peak signal-to-noise ratio over the bands
vx_ms, vy_ms velocity, east and north, m/s
speed_kn, speed_se_kn speed and its standard error, knots
heading_deg heading, degrees clockwise from grid north
axis_diff_deg angle between heading and hull axis, 0-90 degrees
moving 1 when the speed exceeds sigma standard errors and min_speed
wake_behind, wake_ahead NIR brightness behind and ahead (moving vessels)
kin_status ok, few_bands (found in fewer than 3 bands) or edge

The measurement window holds the hull plus three hull lengths on each side; points whose window leaves the computational region are flagged edge. The region must match the band grid.

NOTES

The precision is set by the barycentre of a hull a few pixels long: on Sentinel-2 vessels of 25-40 m, the standard error of the speed is typically 1-3 knots, so only vessels above about 5-8 knots are reliably flagged as moving. Larger hulls are measured better.

The time lags of the MSI vary by up to 2 % across and along the swath (Binet et al. 2022), which is small next to this precision. The registration between the bands is not: the Sentinel-2 requirement is 0.3 pixel at the 3-sigma level and ESA measures 0.07-0.22 pixel for the band pairs (Sentinel-2 Data Quality Report), and 0.2 pixel, 2 m, read as a displacement over 1 s, is about 4 knots. A systematic apparent velocity found on stationary vessels of the same orbit and satellite (for example ships at anchor) can be removed with bias. Registration measured on land is not a substitute: the contrast of vegetation and terrain shadows differs between the visible and near-infrared bands and shifts their apparent position.

The heading is relative to grid north; the difference from true north (the meridian convergence of the projection) is a fraction of a degree near the central meridian of a UTM zone.

Other sensors with a known inter-band delay can be used by giving their bands and time_lags, in the order blue, green, red, near-infrared.

EXAMPLES

Speed and heading of the vessels detected in one Sentinel-2 scene, imported with r.in.sentinel:

r.in.sentinel -o collection=sentinel-2-l2a bands=B02,B03,B04,B08 \
  start=2024-10-20 end=2024-10-21 resolution=10 output=s2
g.region raster=s2_20241020_B08

i.vessel.velocity input=vessels blue=s2_20241020_B02 green=s2_20241020_B03 \
  red=s2_20241020_B04 nir=s2_20241020_B08 output=vessels_velocity \
  length_column=length_m orient_column=orient_deg

v.db.select vessels_velocity columns=cat,speed_kn,speed_se_kn,heading_deg,moving \
  where="moving = 1"
